### Related topics

moire:twisted bilayer

### One-sentence summary

This paper studies the Aharonov-Casher (AC) phase in twisted bilayer graphene, finding that it exhibits sharp peaks at magic angles due to the minimization of Fermi velocity, and proposes an experimental measurement method using double-tip scanning tunneling spectroscopy.

### Problem

To explore how the Aharonov-Casher (AC) geometric phase of electrons in twisted bilayer graphene under a perpendicular electric field is affected by the twist angle (especially magic angles) and renormalized Fermi velocity, and how to measure it experimentally.

### Approach

Using continuum models to calculate the low-energy effective Hamiltonian and renormalized Fermi velocity of twisted bilayer graphene, deriving the SU(2) Berry connection and AC phase, and designing a double-tip scanning tunneling spectroscopy (DTSTS) measurement scheme with ferromagnetic tips.

### Main takeaway

The Aharonov-Casher (AC) phase is directly proportional to the perpendicular electric field strength and inversely proportional to the renormalized Fermi velocity, leading to extremely sharp peaks at the magic angles where the Fermi velocity is minimized.

### Why it matters

This study links geometric phases to the flat-band physics of twisted graphene, providing a microscopic diagnostic tool to directly probe valley Berry curvature, quantum metric, and local electric field or strain variations in moiré superlattices.

### Value assessment

- **Mechanism clarification**

### One-sentence summary

This work reveals how quantum geometry drives the RKKY magnetic exchange from flat to dispersive bands, showing that the correlation length is jointly controlled by the band gap and geometry, and exhibits a unique geometric selection effect.

### Problem

To understand how quantum geometry determines the spatial correlation length of the RKKY magnetic exchange in the absence of a conventional Fermi surface (fully filled flat band), and how restoring dispersion affects RKKY behavior.

### Approach

Using an analytically solvable two-band model combined with pole analysis of band projectors in the complex momentum plane to calculate the RKKY interaction in both flat and dispersive bands and analyze its spatial decay.

### Main takeaway

The RKKY interaction in a filled flat band decays exponentially with a length scale set by both the gap and quantum geometry, where stronger geometry can counterintuitively shorten the range for small gaps; with dispersion, a symmetry-protected geometric selection effect can change the RKKY tail from 1/R2 to 1/R3.

### Why it matters

It challenges the conventional paradigm that a single quantum metric scale controls spatial correlations in flat bands, providing a theoretical foundation for tuning magnetic interactions in materials via moire engineering and gate voltage.

### Value assessment
