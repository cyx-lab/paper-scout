### One-sentence summary

The study shows that a quasiperiodic Qi-Wu-Zhang Chern insulator realizes a persistent critical phase that avoids localization and exhibits anomalous diffusion.

### Problem

To investigate whether a 2D topological Chern insulator under an irrational magnetic field (quasiperiodic modulation) can realize localization physics and eigenstate transitions distinct from conventional scalar models.

### Approach

Reduce the magnetic QWZ model to a spinor quasiperiodic chain with matrix onsite modulation, and analyze it using finite-size scaling, dual transformation, Lyapunov exponents, and wave-packet dynamics.

### Why it matters

It identifies magnetic Chern insulator systems as a natural platform for robust criticality and anomalous quantum transport, providing a new mechanism for non-localizing critical phases in quasiperiodic systems.
