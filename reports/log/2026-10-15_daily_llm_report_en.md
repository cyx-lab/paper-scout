### One-sentence summary

This paper studies the Landau levels of C high-fold fermions in honeycomb lattices, revealing a combination of Dirac-type and anomalous Landau quantization rooted in the quantum geometry of singular flat bands.

### Problem

The authors aim to explore how C high-fold fermions in multi-orbital honeycomb lattices (such as triangulene crystals) develop Landau levels and edge states under a strong perpendicular magnetic field, clarifying the quantization features of Dirac bands versus singular flat bands.

### Approach

Combining full tight-binding models, reduced tight-binding models based on zero modes, and k-p perturbation theory, the authors compute and compare the Landau levels and wave function structures of the (1,1), (1,2), (2,2), and (3,3) models.

### Main takeaway

Multi-orbital honeycomb systems exhibit rich Landau quantization: dispersive Dirac bands follow square-root magnetic scaling, while quadratic band crossings show both conventional linear scaling and anomalous 1/n flat-band Landau levels governed by quantum geometry.

### Why it matters

This work establishes triangulene crystals as a molecular platform for exploring the coexistence of Dirac physics and singular flat bands, bridging molecular architecture with unique magnetic quantization and quantum geometry.
