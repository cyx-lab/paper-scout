### One-sentence summary

This work demonstrates a quantum-interference metrology of microcomb dissipative Kerr solitons based on Hong-Ou-Mandel interference, achieving optical amplification-free and dispersion-immune pulse characterization.

### Problem

Conventional ultrafast pulse characterization of microcomb solitons requires optical amplification and precise dispersion compensation, which introduce waveform distortions and limit reliable measurement of complex multi-soliton states.

### Approach

The soliton stream is attenuated to the single-photon level, and Hong-Ou-Mandel fourth-order interference is measured to directly retrieve intrinsic pulse durations and inter-soliton temporal separations.

### Main takeaway

Quantum-interference metrology bypasses optical amplification and dispersion limits, retrieving an intrinsic soliton pulse width of 83.4 fs (unchanged after 25 km fiber propagation) and mapping multi-soliton structures with sub-picosecond resolution.

### Why it matters

It bridges quantum optics and ultrafast nonlinear photonic dynamics, providing a distortion-free, highly sensitive diagnostic tool for low-power microcombs and complex soliton dynamics.
