### One-sentence summary

Polarized Raman spectroscopy confirms the electronically driven origin of band reconstruction in GdGaI and reveals magnetic-field-induced chiral phonons.

### Problem

To determine whether the band reconstruction in the candidate excitonic insulator GdGaI is driven by electron-electron interactions (excitonic mechanism) or electron-phonon coupling (charge-density-wave mechanism).

### Approach

Combining polarization-resolved Raman spectroscopy (down to 4 K and up to 9 T) with density functional theory (DFT) phonon calculations and symmetry analysis.

### Main takeaway

No evidence of lattice distortion or phonon softening was found upon cooling, confirming an electronically driven band reconstruction, while a pronounced circular dichroism of A1g phonons was observed under magnetic fields due to spin-phonon coupling.

### Why it matters

It provides crucial experimental evidence to distinguish excitonic insulators from charge density waves, and demonstrates circular-polarization Raman as a sensitive probe of spin-phonon coupling and short-range magnetic correlations in magnetic excitonic systems.

### One-sentence summary

This study develops a two-stage analytical method based on the solvable modified Kratzer model to rapidly retrieve fundamental material parameters of monolayer transition-metal dichalcogenides directly from optical and magneto-optical exciton spectra.

### Problem

Conventional methods for extracting material parameters of 2D semiconductors from experimental exciton spectra rely on complex numerical fitting, which is computationally expensive and lacks physical transparency.

### Approach

Based on the analytically solvable modified Kratzer model, a two-stage retrieval framework is established: the first stage analytically calculates the bandgap and screening parameters from the three lowest zero-field exciton states, and the second stage independently determines the effective exciton mass and environmental dielectric constant using analytical magnetoexciton energy expressions.

### Main takeaway

The analytical framework not only accurately retrieves core material parameters for various monolayer TMDCs in different dielectric environments, but also directly predicts diamagnetic coefficients, exciton radii, and complete magnetoexciton spectra without any fitting parameters or matrix diagonalization.

### Why it matters

It provides an efficient, physically transparent, and widely applicable analytical alternative to tedious numerical fitting for the spectroscopic characterization of two-dimensional semiconductors.
