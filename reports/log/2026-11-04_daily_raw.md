**Title:** Altermagnetism-induced higher-order topological Dirac semimetals in two-dimensional nonsymmorphic systems

**Keyword hits:** altermagnetism:altermagnetism, altermagnetism:altermagnetic

**Authors:** Lizhou Liu, Cheng-Ming Miao, Ying-Tao Zhang, Qing-Feng Sun

**Author_match:** False

**Abstract:**
> We propose an altermagnetism-induced route to a two-dimensional higher-order topological Dirac semimetal in a trilayer nonsymmorphic Dirac system. Interlayer hybridization preserves the nonsymmorphic bulk Dirac crossings and generates helical edge states in the trilayer phase. Introducing outer-layer $d$-wave altermagnetic order gaps the helical edge states, while only the symmetry-protected Dirac crossing at high-symmetry point $M$ remains gapless. The resulting phase hosts a surviving bulk Dirac point, gapped edge spectra, and corner states. An exact layer-space decomposition further reveals that these features arise from the coexistence of a gapless Dirac sector and a gapped $\mathbb{Z}_2$ topological sector. Our results establish altermagnetism as an effective mechanism for engineering higher-order topological Dirac semimetals in two-dimensional nonsymmorphic systems.

## Rank #2

**Title:** Emergence of Berry curvature dipole and nonlinear Hall effect in a novel hexagonal borophosphene allotrope

**Keyword hits:** quantum_geometry:berry curvature, quantum_geometry:nonlinear Hall, quantum_geometry:nonlinear Hall effect

**Authors:** Ranjan Kumar Tung, Dimple, Venkatesan Subramanian, Vijay Singh

**Author_match:** False

**Abstract:**
> We investigate the electronic structure, local orbital coupling, and strain-tunable nonlinear Hall response of two arsenic-substituted borophosphene-derived monolayers, As@MBP and As@AMBP, using first-principles calculations and tight-binding analysis. In the absence of spin--orbit coupling, both systems exhibit a finite Berry-curvature dipole (BCD) and a substantial nonlinear Hall conductivity, demonstrating that the nonlinear response is intrinsically driven by orbital physics and inversion-symmetry breaking. $As$ substitution opens sizable band gaps of $120~\mathrm{meV}$ in As@MBP and $90~\mathrm{meV}$ in As@AMBP, well exceeding room-temperature thermal fluctuations ($k_{\mathrm{B}}T \approx 25.9~\mathrm{meV}$). Tight-binding analysis reveals that $As$ incorporation breaks the equivalence of $B-P$ hoppings in As@MBP and induces sign reversals in key nearest-neighbor hoppings in As@AMBP, reflecting a pronounced electronic reconstruction beyond simple geometric effects. Under mechanical strain, the redistribution of Berry curvature yields a maximum BCD of $22~\mathring{\text{A}}$ in As@MBP, approximately $10$ times larger than that of unstrained structure, and induces a reversal of the BCD sign. These results establish $As$-substituted borophosphene derivatives as promising light-element platforms for mechanically switchable nonlinear transport and quantum-material applications without relying on strong spin-orbit coupling.

## Rank #3

**Title:** LaQX: A JAX Toolkit for Neural Quantum State Simulations of Lattice Quantum Many-Body Models

**Keyword hits:** altermagnetism:altermagnetic

**Authors:** Yuntian Gu, Chaohui Fan, Ruichen Li, Haoxiang Chen, Weizhong Fu, Yixiao Chen, Dingshun Lv, Weiluo Ren, Tao Xiang, Liwei Wang, Mingpu Qin, Xiang Li

**Author_match:** False

**Abstract:**
> Neural quantum states provide accurate variational representations for interacting lattice fermions and spins. Turning a new ansatz into a reproducible calculation, however, requires coordinated implementations of the Hamiltonian, sampling, optimization, observables, and accelerator parallelism. We present LaQX (Lattice Quantum simulation based on jaX), an open-source neural quantum state library based on JAX that organizes these components through common functional interfaces. Beyond ground-state variational Monte Carlo, LaQX supports excited-state calculations, Green's Function Monte Carlo refinement, adiabatic evolution, and real-time evolution with time-dependent variational Monte Carlo. It consolidates methods developed in recent high-accuracy neural quantum state studies of the Hubbard model and frustrated magnets, and the package has been used to investigate the triangular-lattice Hofstadter-Hubbard model. We document these capabilities with reproducible calculations for Hubbard, $J_1$-$J_2$ Heisenberg, altermagnetic Hubbard, and interacting Hofstadter models. Function-oriented modules separate the scientific components and limit the source context needed for extensions by human developers and large language models. Source code, documentation, and executable examples are available at https://github.com/bytedance/laqx.

## Rank #4

**Title:** Anomalous Hall Response and Real-Space Current Correlations in Rhombohedral Graphene

**Keyword hits:** quantum_geometry:berry curvature, quantum_geometry:wilson loop

**Authors:** Zhen Bi, Chandra M. Varma

**Author_match:** False

**Abstract:**
> We investigate the anomalous Hall effect (AHE) and magnetization observed in rhombohedral graphene through real-space calculations which lead to new insights. Our calculations reveal no equilibrium loop currents of the type associated with the Haldane model of a quantum anomalous Hall insulator. Instead, the AHE arises in this instance from interference between electronic paths forming oriented closed Wilson loops of current operators in the presence of valley polarization. We show some examples of these Wilson loops. Using the fact that the added-carrier charge density resides dominantly in the top and bottom layers, we develop a real-space expansion of the surface Hamiltonian that reproduces the Hall response of the full model with good accuracy. We also predict that a smooth local potential induces equilibrium circulating currents whose strength depends on both Berry curvature and the orbital moments of states at the Fermi surface.

## Rank #5

**Title:** Nonlinearity-Induced Suppression of Dissipation in Bath-Coupled Floquet Topological Insulators

**Keyword hits:** soliton:soliton

**Authors:** Bhoomija Chaurasia, Sebabrata Mukherjee

**Author_match:** False

**Abstract:**
> Topological insulators can host chiral, disorder-robust edge states that dissipate energy when coupled to an external bath. We show that nonlinear interactions enable all-optical control over edge-state dissipation in a laser-written Floquet topological insulator periodically coupled to a photonic bath. Although moderate nonlinearity enhances dissipation due to mode mixing, increasing the nonlinear strength to an optimal value dramatically suppresses dissipation by forming highly localized traveling edge solitons. These results establish engineered system-bath coupling and nonlinear interactions as a route to suppress dissipation of chiral topological edge transport.
