## Hidden interfacial magnetism in RuO$_2$ heterostructures revealed by a superconducting spin switch

- **summarized_at:** `2026-11-11T11:42:12`

### Related topics

altermagnetism:altermagnetic

### One-sentence summary

Using a superconducting spin switch, researchers reveal hidden weak magnetism at the buried RuO2/Nb interface, originating from an oxygen-vacancy-driven RuOₓ redox layer.

### Problem

The magnetism of bulk RuO2 is controversial; while macroscale measurements show no bulk magnetic order, local surfaces and interfaces often exhibit elusive weak magnetism whose origin and mechanism remain unclear.

### Approach

Constructing a GdN/Nb/RuO2 superconducting spin switch, using the sensitivity of the superconducting transition temperature to probe interfacial exchange fields, combined with quasiclassical Usadel equations and control experiments.

### Main takeaway

Oxygen scavenging at the Nb/RuO2 interface forms a weakly magnetic and magnetically hard RuOₓ layer with random anisotropy, whose exchange field is precisely detected by the superconducting spin switch.

### Why it matters

This work demonstrates the superconducting spin switch as a powerful quantitative tool for probing buried interfacial weak magnetism undetectable by conventional magnetometry, clarifying interface magnetic mechanisms in RuO2 heterostructures.

### Value assessment

- **Mechanism clarification**
- The study successfully clarifies the long-debated microscopic redox and oxygen-vacancy origin of interfacial weak magnetism in RuO2 via clever superconducting device design.

### One-sentence summary

This work uses first-principles calculations to systematically investigate the structural and electronic properties of multiple polymorphs of HfO2, ZrO2, and HZO, demonstrating their impact in TCAD simulations of ferroelectric devices.

### Problem

TCAD device simulations require high-accuracy material parameters, but key material properties for HfO2, ZrO2, and HZO—including metastable ferroelectric phases—remain poorly understood largely due to challenges in synthesizing phase-pure samples.

### Approach

First-principles calculations at increasing levels of theory—from semi-local GGA, meta-GGA, and hybrid functionals to GW approximations—were performed to extract lattice parameters, dielectric constants, and band gaps, combined with TCAD device simulations using the GLK model.

### Main takeaway

Higher-rung functionals significantly outperform PBE for lattice parameters and dielectric constants; band gap differences between phases are consistent across functionals; TCAD simulations show that non-ferroelectric interlayers drastically alter ferroelectric capacitor hysteresis and band profiles.

### Why it matters

This work provides a comprehensive, reliable material parameter database validated across different theoretical rungs, which is crucial for computer-aided design and optimization of ferroelectric-based electronic devices.

### Value assessment

- **Mechanism clarification**

### One-sentence summary

This work reports experimental evidence of anomalous Hall crystals in moiré rhombohedral graphene through electrical transport and noise measurements.

### Problem

The physical origin of Chern insulators in moiré rhombohedral graphene is under debate, lacking direct experimental evidence for anomalous Hall crystals driven by spontaneous electron crystallization at zero magnetic field.

### Approach

Using dual-gated moiré rhombohedral graphene devices combined with electrical transport measurements, bias-annealed protocols, low-frequency noise power spectral density analysis, and systematic temperature and magnetic field studies.

### Main takeaway

Observation of Chern insulators spanning an incommensurate, continuous filling range accompanied by current-induced broadband noise and memory effects, establishing charge-ordered states as parental phases for Chern insulators.

### Why it matters

This study establishes experimental evidence for anomalous Hall crystals—a novel state of matter distinct from reentrant quantum Hall states—and opens doors for exploring collective dynamics in strongly correlated Chern bands.

### Value assessment
