- **summarized_at:** `2026-07-22T09:30:30`

### Related topics

### One-sentence summary

An anomalous photoluminescence enhancement in monolayer MoS2/graphene heterostructures is achieved via polystyrene-assisted wet transfer mediated by interfacial charge transfer.

### Problem

Interlayer charge transfer typically quenches the photoluminescence of monolayer MoS2 in MoS2/graphene heterostructures, limiting their optoelectronic applications.

### Approach

Integrating CVD-grown n-type monolayer MoS2 with graphene using a polystyrene-assisted wet transfer method, and analyzing the interface via spectroscopy, KPFM, XPS, and DFT calculations.

### Main takeaway

The depletion of residual sodium ions and the introduction of interfacial hydroxyl and epoxy groups during wet transfer drive electron transfer from MoS2 to graphene, converting trions to neutral excitons and resulting in a 54-fold PL enhancement.

### Why it matters

It offers a process-compatible and scalable interface-engineering strategy to modulate excitonic emission in 2D heterostructures without external gating or complex pre-functionalization.

### Value assessment

- **Mechanism clarification**
- It clarifies how chemical changes during wet transfer (sodium depletion and oxygen functionalization) cooperatively modulate interfacial charge transfer, explaining the anomalous PL enhancement.

- **summarized_at:** `2026-07-22T09:30:49`

### Related topics

altermagnetism:altermagnetic

### One-sentence summary

This work investigates the pure spin bulk photovoltaic effect in an altermagnetic higher-order topological insulator heterostructure, demonstrating control over topological phases and spin photocurrents via Néel vector rotation.

### Problem

How to describe the spin bulk photovoltaic effect in systems with PT-enforced band degeneracies, and how to generate and control pure spin photocurrents using higher-order topology and altermagnetism.

### Approach

Constructed a 2D TI and d-wave altermagnet heterostructure model, developed a non-Abelian spin BPVE formulation for PT-degenerate manifolds, and numerically calculated spin shift and injection currents under different Néel vector orientations.

### Main takeaway

When the Néel vector is in-plane, the system realizes a second-order topological insulator phase where symmetry completely suppresses charge photocurrents, enabling a pure spin bulk photovoltaic effect (spin shift current via linearly polarized light and spin injection current via circularly polarized light).

### Why it matters

It offers a new scheme for generating pure spin currents without accompanying charge currents, and demonstrates that nonlinear spin transport can sensitively probe band inversion, higher-order topology, and magnetic symmetries.

### Value assessment

- **Major advance**
- Proposed a non-Abelian spin BPVE theory for degenerate bands and demonstrated magnetic control of pure spin photocurrents in an altermagnetic higher-order topological insulator.

### One-sentence summary

This paper investigates the bifurcation and stability of vortex clusters from multipole and second-order ring solitons in cubic-quintic media with a harmonic trapping potential.

### Problem

Unveiling the formation and stabilization mechanisms of vortex clusters in two-dimensional nonlinear media.

### Approach

Modeling with the 2D cubic-quintic nonlinear Schrödinger equation under a harmonic potential, and analyzing soliton dynamics using linear stability analysis and direct numerical simulations.

### Main takeaway

Vortex clusters with up to 4 alternating-charge vortices bifurcate from multipole solitons, while larger clusters bifurcate from second-order ring solitons, with dipole and quadrupole clusters showing stability over a wide range.

### Why it matters

This study provides a theoretical basis for manipulating and stabilizing complex vortex structures in nonlinear optics, Bose-Einstein condensates, and quantum droplets.
