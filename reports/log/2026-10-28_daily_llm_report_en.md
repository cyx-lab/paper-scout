This paper studies topological Chern bands, quantum geometry, and the role of bond connectivity derived from flat-band parents in a seven-orbital honeycomb-vacancy lattice.

### Problem

Strictly local periodic tight-binding models cannot simultaneously support isolated, exactly flat, and Chern-nontrivial bands. ### Related topics

### One-sentence summary

Investigating the SU(3) triangular-lattice Hofstadter-Hubbard model using iDMRG, we find that the transition between an integer quantum Hall insulator and a chiral spin liquid is governed by neutral and charge-3e composite excitations while the single-electron gap remains open.

### Problem

Determining which collective or composite excitations become critical near the phase transition from an electronic insulator to a chiral spin liquid (CSL) while microscopic electrons remain gapped.

### Approach

Employing infinite density matrix renormalization group (iDMRG) calculations to study charge pumping, entanglement spectra, and transfer-matrix correlation lengths in the SU(3) triangular-lattice Hofstadter-Hubbard model at one particle per site and flux pi/3 per triangle.

### Main takeaway

Near the transition between the integer quantum Hall insulator and the SU(3) chiral spin liquid, the neutral and charge-3e correlation lengths increase substantially while the single-electron correlation length remains short, supporting a Chern-Simons-Higgs critical theory with low-energy trion fluctuations.

### Why it matters

This work extends the sector-resolved perspective of topological criticality from SU(2) to an odd-charge, fermionic composite channel (charge-3e trion) and offers potential implications for doping fractional quantum anomalous Hall states in twisted transition metal dichalcogenides.

### Value assessment
