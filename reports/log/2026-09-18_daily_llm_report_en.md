This paper investigates the dielectric response and plasmonic excitations in twisted bilayer graphene across dispersive and flat-band regimes using time-dependent density-functional theory.

### Problem

Understanding the collective charge excitation mechanisms in twisted bilayer graphene as it evolves from large-angle dispersive bands to small-angle quasi-flat bands at the atomistic level.

### Approach

This study provides a microscopic dynamical foundation for understanding electronic correlations and potential plasmon-mediated superconductivity in magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene.
