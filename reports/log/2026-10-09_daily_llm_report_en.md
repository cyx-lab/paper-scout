### One-sentence summary

This paper investigates longitudinal magnon wave-packet dynamics driven by the symplectic quantum metric in a one-dimensional chiral soliton lattice.

### Problem

While quantum metric transport in electronic systems is widely studied, how the symplectic structure and quantum metric govern the longitudinal wave-packet dynamics of bosonic magnons in one-dimensional textured magnets remains unexplored.

### Approach

The authors developed a linear spin-wave model for a one-dimensional chiral soliton lattice and derived semiclassical equations of motion for magnon wave packets under a weak spatial chemical potential perturbation within a pseudo-unitary Bogoliubov-de Gennes framework.
