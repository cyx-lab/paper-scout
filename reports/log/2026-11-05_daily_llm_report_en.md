### One-sentence summary

This paper establishes a general geometric theory of optical work in coherent wave scattering, proving that cyclically modulating multiple parameters generates net optical work determined by the Berry curvature.

### Problem

Previous studies on adiabatic pumping focused on transport between incoherent reservoirs, lacking a unified geometric theory to describe the generation and control of optical work in time-varying photonics and optomechanics.

### Approach

Using a time-varying unitary scattering matrix formalism, the authors derive the optical work formula for infinitesimal parameter variations and transform closed-loop integrals into surface integrals dependent on the Berry curvature via generalized Stokes' theorem.

### Main takeaway

The net optical work accumulated under adiabatic modulation is a geometric quantity requiring at least two independently modulated parameters, increasing with illumination coherence and vanishing under maximally mixed states.

### Why it matters

This framework unifies time-varying photonics, optomechanics, and geometric transport, providing a new avenue for maximizing energy exchange in complex optical systems via wavefront shaping and parameter-space trajectory optimization.

### Value assessment
