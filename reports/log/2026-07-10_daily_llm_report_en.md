### Problem

To clarify the microscopic mechanism of exciton valley relaxation in monolayer TMDs, distinguishing whether it is governed by coherent exchange-driven precession near the light cone (Dyakonov-Perel mechanism) or by exciton-phonon intervalley scattering across the entire Brillouin zone.

### Approach

### Why it matters

It corrects the long-standing attribution of valley relaxation to the Dyakonov-Perel mechanism, provides quantitative temperature-dependent valley depolarization and decoherence times for MoS2, and exposes the representation-dependent artifacts of the widely used Lindblad approximation.
