### Approach

### One-sentence summary

This work develops a vortex-beam-based transient absorption microspectroscopy (V-TAM) platform to resolve the ultrafast diffusion dynamics of free excitons and polarons in 2D perovskites without spatial imaging.

### Problem

Conventional transient absorption microscopy requires complex spatial scanning and image reconstruction, and lacks the temporal sensitivity to resolve early-time nonequilibrium (hot-carrier) diffusion dynamics on sub-picosecond scales.

### Approach

Combining a Gaussian pump with vortex probe beams of different topological charges to encode spatial diffusion into mode-dependent pump-probe signals, analyzed using a time-dependent diffusion model.

### Main takeaway

### Why it matters

### Value assessment
