### One-sentence summary

The study reveals the decisive impact of twist angle on the efficiency of ultrafast charge separation in WS2-graphene heterostructures.

### Problem

How the twist angle influences ultrafast charge transfer across van der Waals interfaces remains controversial and lacks direct microscopic experimental evidence.

### Approach

Using time- and angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (trARPES) to directly probe band evolution and carrier dynamics in WS2-graphene heterostructures with 0° and 30° twist angles after photoexcitation.

### Main takeaway

At a 0° twist angle, hole transfer is significantly faster than electron transfer, leading to efficient charge separation; at 30°, electron and hole transfer occur at comparable rates, resulting in no charge separation.

### Why it matters

The findings demonstrate that the twist angle is a key parameter for tuning interfacial charge dynamics, providing design rules for optimizing 2D material-based photovoltaic and optoelectronic devices.

### Value assessment

### Problem

### Approach
