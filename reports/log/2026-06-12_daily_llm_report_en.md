### Problem

Conventional photonic flat bands are often limited to specific wavevector directions and struggle to simultaneously achieve high Q-factors, strong chirality, and omnidirectional angular robustness.

### Approach

Utilizing a square-lattice silicon membrane metasurface, the authors weaken coupling between Bloch modes by increasing the lattice period to achieve band flattening, and introduce chirality by breaking the mirror symmetry of the air holes.

### Main takeaway

### Why it matters

This work establishes a general paradigm for engineering omnidirectional chiral flat bands on planar silicon platforms, significant for nonlinear nanophotonics, chiral light-matter interactions, and integrated devices.

### Value assessment

### One-sentence summary

Real-time spatiotemporal visualization of 3D soliton dynamics in multimode fiber lasers is achieved using array-based spatial sampling and time-division multiplexing.

### Problem

Conventional detection techniques struggle to record the evolution of 3D solitons in multimode fiber lasers with simultaneous high spatiotemporal resolution over long durations.

### Approach

Proposed array-based spatial sampling with time-division multiplexing (ASTM) combined with dispersive Fourier transformation (DFT) to reconstruct roundtrip-resolved beam profiles and spectra using high-speed photodetectors.

### Main takeaway

During the birth of 3D solitons, transverse modes stabilize and lock significantly before the onset of spectral broadening (longitudinal mode locking), revealing a new mechanism in the buildup of spatiotemporal mode-locking.

### Why it matters

This study provides a critical diagnostic tool for high-dimensional nonlinear dynamics and offers physical guidance for the design of high-performance ultrafast multimode lasers.

### Value assessment

- **Major advance**
- Developed a novel real-time spatiotemporal imaging method and provided the first direct observation of transverse modes locking before longitudinal modes during 3D soliton formation.
