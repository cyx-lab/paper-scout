This paper presents a parsimonious structure model for the icosahedral Cd-Yb quasicrystal using only spherical and elliptical occupation domains to significantly reduce computational cost.

### Problem

### Approach

### Related topics

quantum_geometry:circular dichroism

### One-sentence summary

Proposes a spectroscopic scheme to directly measure Hall viscosity in quantum Hall systems using circular dichroism induced by rotating quadrupolar perturbations.

### Problem

Hall viscosity is a key geometric property of quantum Hall states, but it remains notoriously difficult to measure directly due to its coupling to metric deformations.

### Approach

Uses rotating saddle potentials as chiral metric-sensitive drives to extract Hall viscosity from the differential absorption (circular dichroism) and employs frequency-resolved spectroscopy to isolate the signal.

### Main takeaway

The integrated dichroic signal is directly proportional to the Hall viscosity, and the method remains robust in lattice systems and finite geometries via a local marker formulation.

### Why it matters

It provides a practical spectroscopic route to probe intrinsic orbital spin and quantum geometry of topological phases in cold-atom and quantum simulation platforms.

### Value assessment

- **Major advance**
- Provides a concrete experimental protocol for measuring the elusive Hall viscosity with broad applicability across different platforms.

### One-sentence summary

This work demonstrates the first experimental and numerical all-optical synchronization of breather solitons in a Kerr microresonator, achieving significant reduction in breathing noise.

### Problem

How to achieve passive synchronization and control of the oscillation frequency of breather solitons in Kerr microresonators using an all-optical approach.

### Approach

Injecting a weak auxiliary continuous-wave laser close to the breathing sidebands in a silicon nitride microresonator, and analyzing the locking behavior via experiments and Lugiato-Lefever equation simulations.

### Main takeaway

The breathing sidebands of breather solitons can be all-optically locked to an injected weak laser, reducing breathing noise and inducing sympathetic shifts in the comb lines.

### Why it matters

It provides a new route to stabilize and control breather solitons, enabling applications in metrology and optical computing, and deepening the understanding of oscillating dissipative structures far from equilibrium.

### Value assessment

- **Major advance**

### One-sentence summary

This work studies the bifurcation structure of soliton self-injection locking in microresonators, revealing the mechanism for deterministic single-soliton generation via feedback parameters.

### Problem

The existence and stability ranges of self-injection locked soliton states under experimental control parameters are not fully understood, making reliable single-soliton generation difficult.

### Approach

Perform bifurcation analysis and numerical continuation on a coupled Lugiato-Lefever equation and laser model in the weak-backscattering limit, validated by direct numerical simulations.

### Main takeaway

Self-injection locking introduces soliton-number-dependent bifurcation structures, yielding parameter regions where only the single soliton is stable, which can be dynamically accessed by sweeping detuning and feedback phase.

### Why it matters

It provides theoretical guidance for designing hybrid integrated soliton microcombs, facilitating deterministic single-soliton generation for low-noise coherent combs.

### Value assessment
