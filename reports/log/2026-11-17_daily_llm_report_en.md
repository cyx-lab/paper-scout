This review surveys excitonic magnetism and competing valence bond solid instabilities in spin-orbit entangled d4 ruthenium compounds.

### Problem

### Related topics

### One-sentence summary

Achieved high-Q and high-efficiency intrinsic circular dichroism via the strong coupling effect in silicon-based bound states in the continuum metasurfaces.

### Problem

Conventional chiral metasurfaces often rely on weak chiral modes or extrinsic pseudo-chirality, lacking high-Q and high-efficiency intrinsic chiral responses independent of excitation conditions.

### Approach

Designed a silicon-based cross-shaped nanohole array metasurface, utilizing under-etching to break out-of-plane symmetry and tuning in-plane asymmetry to induce strong coupling between quasi-BIC and guided-mode resonance modes.

### Main takeaway

The strong coupling mechanism induces anti-crossing behavior between quasi-BIC and GMR modes, achieving a maximum intrinsic CD of 0.995 with a high Q-factor of 1083 in the communication band.

### Why it matters

This work offers a novel design strategy and robust structural scheme for high-performance integrated chiral photonic devices, chiral sensing, chiral lasers, and nonlinear chiral optics.
