### Problem

Intrinsic dissipation and measurement-induced spectral broadening often conceal hybridized light-matter states, preventing Rabi splitting from being resolved in conventional scattering spectra.

### Approach

Maps spectral broadening to temporal decay using space-time Fourier correspondence, and compensates for this decay in the time domain via a Wiener-regularized temporal point-spread function to reconstruct intrinsic spectra.

### Main takeaway

### Why it matters
